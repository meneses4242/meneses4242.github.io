🎯 What This Tool Does

Imagine you posted a job on LinkedIn and received 200 applications. Now you need to review each one. Manually clicking through every profile, downloading every resume, and keeping track of everyone - that takes hours, maybe days.

LinkedIn Applicants MCP does this work for you automatically. It uses your own Chrome browser to visit each applicant, collect their information, download their resumes, and save everything neatly on your computer. It even paces itself like a real person would, so LinkedIn doesn't flag your account.

💡 Key Benefit: You get all your applicant data in one place - lists, resumes, profiles - without spending hours on repetitive clicking.

⚡ Main Features

📋 Applicant Lists

Get a clean, organized list of everyone who applied to your job. Names, dates, statuses - all in one place.

📄 Resume Downloads

Every applicant's resume is automatically downloaded and saved to your computer. No more clicking each one separately.

👤 Profile Snapshots

Get a copy of each applicant's public profile information, so you can compare candidates side by side.

🖥️ Local Dashboard

Everything is organized in a simple, visual dashboard that runs on your computer. No cloud, no sharing your data with anyone.

🐢 Human-Like Pacing

The tool works at a natural speed, with realistic pauses and timing. Your LinkedIn account stays safe.

🔒 Privacy First

Everything runs through your own Chrome browser. Your data never leaves your computer.

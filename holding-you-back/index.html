The 4 Gaps

Why smart, hard-working people stall

Results come from what you do consistently over weeks, not from your best days. When progress stalls, it is almost always one of four gaps between what you think you are doing and what is actually happening.

Your flat weight is your maintenance. If your weekly average weight has not moved for 3+ weeks, whatever you are eating right now is your maintenance. To gain, eat above it. To lose, eat below it. You do not need a formula to start.

      SLP

      Sleep, Stress and Water

      The quiet levers
      Sleep

      Short sleep raises intake

      A review of 11 studies found that after short sleep, people ate about 385 calories more the next day, with no extra calories burned. Aim for 7 to 9 hours.

      Stress

      Plan for hard days

      Pick a non-food reset: a walk, a shower, a call. Have your good-enough meal ready for tough days.

      Water

      Drink through the day

      Thirst and hunger can feel alike. A glass of water before meals is a simple habit.

      Steps

      Move more every day

      Walk after meals when you can. It helps the weekend, the Guesser audit and the scale.

        When to Get Help

        This guide has limits

        This is general education, not medical advice or treatment. Please talk to a doctor or registered dietitian first if you:

        • Are pregnant or nursing
        • Have diabetes, a heart condition or another medical condition
        • Take medication that affects appetite or weight
        • Have a history of an eating disorder, or restrict, binge or purge
        • Feel anxious or out of control around food
        Support: The National Alliance for Eating Disorders helpline is 866-662-1235 (weekdays 9 to 7 ET), and findEDhelp.com lists treatment options. It is not a 24/7 crisis line. For a crisis, call or text 988.
